Bryce Canyon

In this project, I had to create content for the Bryce Canyon National Park. This project was a school trip with some UX design students. We took photos and videos of rangers, the landscape, vistas, tourists, etc. Unfortunately, the weather was stormy throughout the entire trip so my team and I were very limited in what we could accomplish. To circumvent this issue, we tried to shoot content while driving and staying in the driest areas we could find. During this time, I took photos of tourists, parking lots/drivers, and the interior of shops. In the brief moments it wasnt raining, we explored the park as much as we could. During this time, I shot photos of tourists and landscapes. From this project, I got to practice my photoshop and photography skills. At the same time, I learned how to adapt to unforeseen conditions and how to be a positive team player.

Home Improvement Assignment

In this project, I had to build a home improvement calculator. I had to create a site that calculated the amount of paint and carpet the user would need. First, I created a form with a submit button that asked the user for their room width, depth, wall height, and the paint quality they desired. Second, I linked it to a Javascript file that gathered user input, had calculations, and generated a list. I did this by creating equations that utilized the user input to calculate the amount of paint and carpet they would need. Third, I displayed it in a message that only appeared once the user hit the submit button. Other requirements I had to follow included creating a hero image, creating a nav bar, and saving the site to the homescreen of my computer like an app. This project was a struggle because there were a lot of requirements to meet and I have difficulty with javascript. Fortunately, it gave me the opportunity to practice my coding skills and taught me how to create a dynamic site. Organizing and creating notes for my code helped make things understandable. More importantly, the notes guided me in presenting the dynamic information in a clear, responsive layout.
